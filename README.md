# 🩺 Cancer Dataset Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project leverages advanced analytics and global healthcare data to uncover meaningful insights into cancer care, patient outcomes, treatment costs, and health disparities. Using a comprehensive dataset of **50,000 cancer patient records** collected across multiple countries from **2015–2024**, the project aims to transform raw healthcare data into actionable knowledge that can support research, policy-making, and healthcare decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Vision

Cancer remains one of the leading causes of mortality worldwide. Understanding the factors that influence cancer severity, survival outcomes, and treatment costs is essential for improving patient care.

This project bridges the gap between data and decision-making by applying **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**, **statistical analysis**, and **insight generation** to identify patterns, trends, and relationships within a large-scale cancer dataset.

---

## 📊 Dataset Description

The dataset provides a 360-degree view of cancer patient profiles and includes the following categories:

### 👤 Demographics
- Age
- Gender
- Country
- Year of Diagnosis

### 🧬 Genetic & Lifestyle Risk Factors
- Genetic Predisposition
- Smoking Status
- Alcohol Consumption
- Obesity

### 🌍 Environmental Exposure
- Air Pollution Exposure

### 🏥 Clinical & Economic Variables
- Cancer Type
- Cancer Stage
- Treatment Cost

### 📈 Patient Outcomes
- Survival Years
- Severity Scores

---

## 🎯 Objectives

### 1️⃣ Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

The primary goal is to understand the structure and characteristics of the dataset through visualization and statistical exploration.

#### Key Activities
- Analyze distributions of patient demographics
- Identify trends across countries and years
- Explore relationships between lifestyle factors and cancer severity
- Visualize treatment costs across cancer stages
- Compare survival outcomes across patient groups
- Detect hidden patterns and anomalies

---

### 2️⃣ Inferential & Predictive Analytics

Using statistical techniques, we aim to answer critical healthcare-related questions.

#### Research Questions

- What is the relationship between lifestyle risk factors and cancer severity?
- What proportion of patients are diagnosed at early stages for different cancer types?
- Which factors are the strongest predictors of cancer severity?
- Which factors significantly impact survival years?
- How does treatment cost vary across demographics and countries?
- Is higher treatment expenditure associated with improved survival outcomes?
- Do advanced cancer stages result in higher treatment costs and lower survival years?
- Does genetic risk amplify the impact of smoking on severity and survival outcomes?

---

### 3️⃣ Insight Extraction & Documentation

For every major analysis:

- Generate clear, data-driven insights
- Document findings systematically
- Connect results to real-world healthcare implications
- Highlight recommendations for researchers, policymakers, and healthcare organizations
